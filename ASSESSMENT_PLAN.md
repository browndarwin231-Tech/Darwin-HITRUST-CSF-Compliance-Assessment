# Assessment Plan — Nova Care Cloud (Fictional)

**Objective:** Conduct an internal, educational gap review of selected security practices commonly relevant to healthcare security assurance.

**Scope:** Identity and access, security awareness, vulnerability management, incident response, encryption, vendor risk, and backup recovery. Evidence window: fictional records dated January–October 2026. All company systems, users, vendors and records are invented.

**Method:**
1. Establish an *internal practice control objective* and responsible owner.
2. Request a dated, attributable evidence artifact.
3. Inspect the sample and compare it against a documented **fictional company policy** (account disablement in 24h; critical vulnerability remediation in 15 calendar days; high-risk vendor reviews current).
4. Mark Pass, Fail, or Not Tested, including rationale and evidence ID.
5. Write findings for exceptions or missing documentation, assign owners/dates, and prepare an executive summary.
6. Re-test remediated controls after new evidence arrives.

**Decision rule:** 'Missing evidence' is 'Not Tested', not automatically proof of a failed control.

**Limitations:** This is *not* a licensed HITRUST CSF control mapping or full assessment. No official HITRUST requirement IDs, evaluation criteria, scoring, validated assessment, external assessor, PHI, or patient records are used.
